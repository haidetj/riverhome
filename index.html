The written read

RiverHome can have Claude write the issue's prose. Paste an Anthropic API key to turn it on. The key is stored only on this device — it's never uploaded, shared, or committed anywhere.

Get one at console.anthropic.com. It stays on your phone; clear it any time.

Your river spot

RiverHome reads your stretch from live public data. Point it at a USGS stream gauge and set the location.

Auto-filled from the gauge when you pick one. Tap 📍 to use your device location.

Every river runs at its own scale. If you know roughly what "normal" and "too high" look like at this gauge, the ratings get much sharper. Leave blank and RiverHome judges against the last 7 days.

Richer reads with Claude — optional

The numbers, ratings, and advisories work without a key. Add your Anthropic key and Claude writes the daily read and grounds an ecology brief on the species actually seen near you.

Gauge numbers: find yours on the USGS Water Dashboard. Your key stays on this device and is sent only to Anthropic.
